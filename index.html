VEIL rules

48 CARDS
3 cards per player. The four 7s are removed.
Card order: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2. The 2 has King strength and is ignored in number-point calculation.
For number-card points, add the number cards and keep the last digit. Face cards are ignored for this calculation.
A stronger challenge wins the pot and ends the hand. Equal-strength challenge: split the amount played and end the hand.
Folded players cannot win; their committed points stay in the pot. The winner deals next. The dealer acts last; first action begins clockwise from the dealer.
Points are for the current game only. The game ends when one player remains.

Prototype note: some VEIL rules and online play still need implementation and testing. This screen does not claim to be the complete online game.
